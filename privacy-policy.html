Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026
Privacy Policy Terms of Service Data Deletion

Your data is used only by the Hospital. We never publish your details on the internet, and we never sell them or share them for advertising. The only outside services that handle your details are the two listed below: one stores our records and the other delivers our messages.

What we collect

When you register, we record your name, mobile number, age, gender and place. Your age, gender and place are kept for hospital records only.

How we use it

We use your name and mobile number only to send you WhatsApp messages about your visits:

  • a welcome message when you register
  • a confirmation when your follow-up appointment is booked
  • a reminder on the day of your follow-up appointment

Services that handle your data

  • Google Drive: Your records are stored on the Hospital's computers and in the Hospital's own Google Drive account. Only Hospital staff can access them.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.): To deliver a message, we send WhatsApp your mobile number and the message text. The message text includes your name and, where relevant, your appointment date. WhatsApp handles this information under its own business terms.

Stopping messages or deleting your data

To stop receiving messages, or to have your details removed, tell our reception desk or call us. You can also block our number in WhatsApp at any time. Your treatment will not be affected. See our Data Deletion Instructions.
