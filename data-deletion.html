Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 29 October 2026
Privacy Policy Terms of Service Data Deletion

You can ask us at any time to stop WhatsApp messages and delete your contact details and any documents you sent us on WhatsApp. Your treatment will not be affected.

How to request deletion

  1. Visit our reception desk, or call us on 07252-234470.
  2. Give your name and the mobile number you registered with.
  3. Ask for your WhatsApp messaging data, and any documents you sent us, to be deleted.

What happens next

  • We stop all WhatsApp messages to your number.
  • We delete your mobile number and message history from our messaging records within 30 days.
  • We delete any documents you sent us on WhatsApp straight away, and their backup copies within 5 days. Documents are deleted automatically 30 days after you send them in any case.
  • We keep your medical records only as long as the law requires.
